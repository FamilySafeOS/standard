# The FamilySafe OS Manifesto (v1.2)
**Lead Author:** David Emanuel Oprea

## The Twelve Principles

1.  **Vendor Neutrality:** Policies must be portable across all FamilySafe-certified platforms.
2.  **Decisional Hierarchy:** The household administrator is the ultimate authority.
3.  **API Transparency:** OS vendors must provide official, native policy hooks.
4.  **Privacy by Design:** Local processing and E2EE for all synchronization.
5.  **Simplicity as Security:** Setup via the **QR Trust-Pairing** model.
6.  **Offline Sovereignty:** Full governance must function in LAN-only environments.
7.  **Delegated Enrollment:** Mobile satellites act as secure proxies for setup.
8.  **Physical Safety Primacy (Break-Glass):** Audited emergency access always supersedes digital rules.
9.  **Kernel-Level Authority:** Enforcement must be a native, tamper-proof OS subsystem.
10. **Native Integration:** Evolution from middleware hacks to native kernel citizens.
11. **Ecosystem Independence:** Freedom to mix hardware brands without losing governance.
12. **Environmental Totality:** Policies follow the *user*, extending to TVs, Consoles, and Handhelds.
